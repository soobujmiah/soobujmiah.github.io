METHOD OVER TOOL LISTS

Test what matters.
Write down what changed.

The tool depends on the job. My consistent practice is to keep source, build context, test result and limitation close enough to compare.

01

Reproducible builds and automation where they reduce hidden steps.

02

Physical-device testing for Android behavior that a desktop build cannot prove.

03

Separate verified, experimental, planned and unknown states.

04

Documentation that records the failure as well as the successful route.
